상세한 설명

Player rating = average of the Best 15 chart ratings (missing slots count as 0).

Per-chart rating is piecewise linear over acc = score / 1,000 (percent): acc 80 or below → 0 acc 80 ~ 95 → level × g(acc), g: 85→0.20, 90→0.50, 92.5→0.70, 95→1.00 acc 95 ~ 100 → level + mod(acc), mod: 97→+1.5, 99→+2.5 (S, cap), 100→+2.6 (AP) So 95% == the chart's level ("적정"), and the bonus above it is level-independent.

Online ratings combine server scores with the client's registry, including uninstalled charts. Offline ratings retain the existing local score and scanned-chart fallback.
